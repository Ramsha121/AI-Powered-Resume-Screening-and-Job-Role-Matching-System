                                            🔥🔥AI-Powered Resume Insights🔥🔥

AI-Powered Resume Insights is a modern web application that analyzes your resume using AI and NLP. Upload your resume in PDF format and instantly get:
  🧠 Extracted Skills from your resume
  📊 ATS Resume Score to see how your resume ranks
  💡 Word Cloud of key terms
  🎯 Best Job Role Match with clickable links to job listings
  📉 Skill Gap Analysis highlighting areas to improve
This tool helps job seekers optimize their resumes and discover the roles that best match their skills. Built with Python, Streamlit, NLP, and data visualization.
